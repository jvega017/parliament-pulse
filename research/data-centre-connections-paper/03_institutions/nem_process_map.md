# NEM connection process map for large data-centre loads

**Status:** skeleton with verified anchors; completed by the Institutional Economics Agent in Phase 1.

## Structural facts (evidence-linked)

- Over 98 per cent of existing Australian data centre consumption is distribution-connected; about 1.5 per cent transmission-connected (CLM-0014, pending promotion). The stock is a DNSP story.
- The new large-project pipeline is increasingly transmission-level: 11 projects above 5 MW totalling 5.4 GW in the transmission connection process at Q1 2026, about 60 per cent NSW and 40 per cent Victoria (CLM-0017; confirm against AEMO QED primary).
- Observed application-to-energisation timeframe about two years; AEMO assumes a 5 to 10 year ramp (CLM-0017).
- There is no single load-connection queue and no centrally allocated tradable capacity block; connections are bilateral with the relevant NSP.
