## Analytical note

Australia has technical standards and a soft screen, but no allocation or pricing mechanism for scarce connection capacity. That vacuum is the paper's institutional hook. The Texas fee, GB gate, and Irish obligation are three distinct answers to the same screening problem; the model's mechanism set maps onto them (plan v2.0 section 5, Phase 4).
