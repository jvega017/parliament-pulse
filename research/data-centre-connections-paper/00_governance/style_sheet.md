# Style sheet

Binding for all prose artefacts (manuscript, briefs, public derivatives, review responses).

1. Australian English (organise, programme where conventional, licence/license distinction, -ise).
2. Plain, sceptical academic prose. No rhetorical inflation, no generic AI filler, no unsupported superlatives.
3. No em dashes. Use commas, colons or parentheses.
4. Reference style: APA 7 for the working paper. Journal style per the Gate H1 decision for the submission version. One source of truth: `06_manuscript/references.bib`.
5. Claim discipline: every material claim carries a Claim ID and class (factual, model output, assumption, inference, normative). Scenario-dependent figures are labelled scenario-dependent in text at first use.
6. Numbers: report units and periods; distinguish GW requested, GW connected and GW coincident draw explicitly every time; never let "capacity" float between meanings.
7. "Data centres under AI-driven demand uncertainty", never "LLM data centres".
8. Figures readable in grayscale and accessible to colour-vision-deficient readers.
9. Cut, don't compress: length reductions remove secondary material rather than densifying every paragraph.
10. Positive findings and normative recommendations are separated structurally, not blended in a sentence.
